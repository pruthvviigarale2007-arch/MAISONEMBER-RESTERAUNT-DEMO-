ESPACES & SCÉNOGRAPHIE

An Intimate Room Carved in 17th-Century Limestone.

Thirty-four covers configured around our cast-iron hearth, flanked by a brushed brass counter and candlelit alcoves at 14 Rue des Tournelles. Here, warmth is not an aesthetic afterthought—it is the governing material of our hospitality.

SEATING ZONE 01

The Hearth Counter

Eight front-row high oak stools directly overlooking the iron hearth. Watch Chef Alexandre and his brigade tend the live coals, smoke marrow butter, and carve dry-aged cuts before your eyes.

Capacity: 8 Guests · Ideal for couples, solo gastronomes, and tasting menus.

Main Dining Room & Banquettes

Spacious olive-green velvet and saddle leather banquettes bathed in beeswax candlelight. Restrained acoustic architecture creates an unhurried haven for conversation over long feasts.
