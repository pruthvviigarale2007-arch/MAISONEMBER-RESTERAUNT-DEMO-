ROYAL MARATHA HERITAGE

Why Our Tambda & Pandhra Rassa is Legendary

In Kolhapur, rassa is not just a gravy—it is an art form. Unlike commercial restaurants that use food colors or heavy cream, our broths are brewed in traditional brass handis:

  • 🔴 Tambada Rassa: Slow-simmered bone broth with Byadgi chilies, cloves, black pepper, and toasted coconut paste. Spicy, aromatic, and invigorating.
  • ⚪ Pandhra Rassa: Silky white broth extracted from fresh coconut milk, white poppy seeds, cashew nuts, and roasted whole spices. Soothes the palate after the fiery Tambda.
  • 🌿 Solkadhi: Chilled digestive concoction made with real kokum agal, fresh coconut milk, and crushed garlic.
