OUR PHILOSOPHY

Cooking over embers since 2019.

We do not use gas ranges or circulating water baths. Every cut of meat, winter brassica, and crustacean is exposed directly to hardwood coals of sessile oak and charred fruitwood sourced from sustainably managed groves in Picardy.

Our sourdough is fermented on-site for thirty-six hours using ancient stoneground flours from Seine-et-Marne, paired with cultured butter churned and smoked over hazelnut twigs twice weekly.

The Natural Cellar

Curated by head sommelier Camille Roy, our subterranean cellar shelters 420 references dedicated solely to low-intervention, biodynamic growers across the Loire, Jura, and northern Rhône. Unfiltered, honest expressions of living soil.
